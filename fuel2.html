Assumes you refueled to max autonomy at the start, and fuel price is constant. Trip cost can exceed refill cost if you also consumed part of the initial fuel (valued at the same €/km).

Formula
km_added = max - initial
€/km = fuel_cost / km_added
km_used = max - end
trip_cost = km_used * €/km
